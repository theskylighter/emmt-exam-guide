# EMMT Endsem Study Guide

**Magnetic materials and semiconductor devices, told as one story: from a single spinning electron to the hard disk, the transistor and the solar panel.** Every figure from the textbooks is reproduced exactly as printed.

## What the endsem covers

| Source | Topics | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Teacher's list | Construction and working of the **p-n junction diode**, **transistors**, and the **solar cell with J-V characteristics** | high |
| Assignments | **Soft vs hard** magnetic materials and the **hysteresis B-H loop**; **Curie temperature**; **three industrial applications** of magnetic materials | high |
| Midsem syllabus | Quantum numbers, conduction, bands, doping, Hall effect, crystal growth, dielectrics | about 20% |

## Why this order?

The two big topics share one ancestor: **the electron**.

```text
ELECTRON
  |
  +-- spin ---------> MAGNETISM (Ch 1-2)         1600 to 1984
  |
  +-- energy bands -> DOPING (Ch 3)
                        |
                        v
                      p-n JUNCTION  (1940)
                      TRANSISTOR    (1947-48)
                      SOLAR CELL    (1954)       (Ch 4)
```

- Magnetism needs only the **atom** (spin and unpaired 3d electrons), and it came first in history, so it comes first here.
- The devices need **bands and doping**, so the midsem recap of those (Chapter 3) sits right before them, where you need it.
- Midsem topics are woven into the story instead of being a separate chore, which covers the 20% share as you go.

## How every lesson is laid out

1. **The problem**: what people could not explain or build yet.
2. **The idea in plain words**, then an **analogy**.
3. **How it works**, step by step, with the textbook figure.
4. **Key takeaways**, **common mistakes** and **how it is asked in exams**.
5. An **exam-ready definition** and **quick-check questions** with hidden answers.

Lessons tagged **Assignment** or **Teacher list** are the ones most likely to appear as long answers.
